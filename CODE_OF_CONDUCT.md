# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to create an environment where every person feels valued and respected, regardless of their background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Asking questions when uncertain rather than making assumptions
- Giving credit to the work of others

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxing) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Dismissing or invalidating others' concerns or contributions

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions related to this project, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Community forums and discussions
- Email communications
- In-person and virtual events
- Any other spaces where community members interact

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported by contacting the project maintainers at kaiyao28@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

### Enforcement Actions

Upon receipt of a report, project maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge receipt of the report
2. Investigate the reported behavior
3. Contact the person(s) involved to gather information
4. Take appropriate action, which may include:
   - Asking for a clarification or apology
   - Issuing a warning
   - Temporary suspension of participation
   - Permanent removal from the project community

## Our Pledge in Practice

**For reporters:** We take all reports seriously. We encourage you to report concerns early and respectfully. You can report anonymously if you prefer, and we will protect your identity.

**For contributors:** We ask that you assume good intent from others. Most conflicts arise from miscommunication rather than malice. If something offends you, consider asking for clarification before escalating.

**For maintainers:** We commit to responding promptly, investigating fairly, and enforcing this code consistently. We will explain our decisions transparently.
